## 6. Stage Five: Your First Spot Trade (Seven Steps)

### Step 1: Choose a trading pair

Search for the asset you want on the trade page. For Bitcoin, the common pair is **BTC/USDT**, meaning **you use USDT to buy BTC**.

### Step 2: Read the order book

The book shows two sides:

| Position | Name | Meaning |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Top | Sell orders | Prices others want to sell at (what you buy into) |
| Bottom | Buy orders | Prices others want to buy at (who you sell to) |

**For a buy, reference the lowest sell price; for a sell, reference the highest buy price.** The gap between them is the spread.

### Step 3: Choose the order type

For a first trade, use a **market order** or the platform's quick-buy feature to learn the flow.

### Step 4: Enter a quantity or an amount

Most interfaces let you switch between "by quantity" and "by amount":

- By quantity: enter 0.001 BTC
- By amount: enter 100 USDT

**Beginners should use "by amount,"**&#8203; because what you actually want to control is how much money you commit.

### Step 5: Review the fee and estimated result

Before submitting, check two things:
1. **The estimated fee**
2. **How much of the asset you will receive**

### Step 6: Submit the order

Market orders usually fill immediately; limit orders join the queue and wait.

### Step 7: Confirm the fill

Check the Orders page or the Assets page:

| Check | What you should see |
|:---|:---|
| Order status | Completed (or partially filled) |
| Asset balance | Your purchased asset increases |
| Quote balance | Your USDT decreases (including the fee) |

**A partial fill means the rest is still resting** — you can cancel it or wait.

## 8. After You Buy: Checking Holdings and Closing

### Checking holdings

The Assets page shows each asset's balance, and where the interface supports it, a total portfolio value.

### How to close (sell)

Selling mirrors buying:

1. Return to the trade page and select **the same pair**
2. Switch to **Sell**
3. Enter the quantity to sell (often a one-click "max" is available)
4. Choose market or limit and submit
5. Confirm the fill on the Orders page and the USDT change on the Assets page

**Note:**&#8203; a buy incurs one fee and **the sell incurs another**. A round trip is two fees — which is why frequent in-and-out trading costs more than intuition suggests.
