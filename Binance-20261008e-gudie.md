## 1. Why Your First Exchange Matters

Beginners often assume they can just switch later. In practice, your first exchange carries three hidden costs:

1. **Learning cost.** Order types, maker/taker fees, take-profit and stop-loss settings — you learn all of it on your first platform. Switching later means learning again, and many people simply never switch, even when the platform is not ideal.
2. **Security cost.** The early stage is when your holdings are most concentrated and your security awareness is lowest. If a platform lacks two-factor authentication, an anti-phishing code, and withdrawal whitelists, your funds sit behind the weakest defenses.
3. **Fee cost.** A referral code can usually be attached **only at sign-up**. Open a random account first, and you generally cannot attach the code later — you would have to register again. Over time, that fee gap compounds.

In short, your first exchange is not a trial run. It is where you build habits, lock in fee rates, and store assets.
