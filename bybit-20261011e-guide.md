## 1. What Is the Bybit Card?

In one sentence: **the Bybit Card is a crypto payment card linked to your Bybit account**, letting you spend directly at merchants that accept its card network, funded by the crypto assets in your account (for example, USDT).

Three key points:

1. **It is a payment tool, not an investment product.** The card simply connects the assets in your account to online and offline spending. It does not earn money for you.
2. **It spends coins, not fiat.** At checkout, the system deducts the equivalent value in crypto from your account. If the funding asset is a stablecoin like USDT, the experience is closest to "spending in dollars."
3. **Availability depends on your region.** The card network, issuing partner, whether a physical card exists, and which regions are supported all vary — **always defer to the official Bybit pages and what your region shows**.

## 2. How It Works: From Swipe to Settlement

There are really only three steps:

1. **Link.** The card is connected to your Bybit account, with a designated funding source (usually crypto in your funding account).
2. **Spend.** Pay at a merchant terminal or online, or add it to a mobile wallet such as Apple Pay or Google Pay where supported.
3. **Settle.** The system deducts the equivalent value in crypto from your account under the applicable rules.

**The key idea:**&#8203; what you spend is the asset's value *at that moment*. If the funding asset is a stablecoin like USDT, the amount is relatively stable; if it is a volatile token, the actual quantity deducted moves with price.
