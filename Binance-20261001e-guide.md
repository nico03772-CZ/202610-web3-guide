## 1. What Is Grid Trading?

The core logic is simple: **place a ladder of evenly spaced buy and sell orders within a set price range**.

- Price falls to a buy level → auto-buy.
- Price rises to a sell level → auto-sell.
- Price oscillates → trades trigger repeatedly, capturing the spread on each grid step.

**In one line**: grid trading profits from **volatility**, not from **direction**. The more the price oscillates, the more often it fills.

### Which market conditions suit grids?

| Condition | Grid performance |
|:---|:---|
| Range-bound | ✅ Ideal — repeated triggers capture spreads |
| Slow uptrend | ⚠️ Still works, but gradually sells holdings and may miss later gains |
| Sharp downtrend | ❌ Worst case — keeps buying into a falling market |
| Sharp uptrend | ⚠️ Sells out of inventory quickly; limited gains but no loss |

**Key realization**: grids aren't a guaranteed-profit tool — **they lose in one-way downtrends too**, just in the form of holding a pile of depreciating coins.
