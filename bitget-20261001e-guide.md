## 3. Where Does the Yield Come From?

Understanding the source is how you judge the risk:

1. **Lending interest**: your assets are lent to leveraged traders or institutions, and you earn the spread.
2. **On-chain yield**: real returns from staking, validators, or liquidity provision.
3. **Platform subsidies**: temporary incentives to attract capital — this portion can end at any time.
4. **Market-making and strategy returns**: profit shares from platform or third-party strategies.

**The test**: if a product's rate is clearly above its peers, ask where the yield comes from. High yield with no clear source usually hides risk below the waterline.

## 6. Four Practical Principles for Choosing Products

1. **Match liquidity needs first**: flexible for anything possibly needed soon; fixed only for funds you won't touch.
2. **Higher yield means higher risk**: an iron rule — when a rate looks unusually high, find the risk disclosure.
3. **Diversify**: don't put everything into one product or one coin.
4. **Watch the coin price, not just the rate**: coin-denominated yield can be erased by a price decline — what matters is the **net return in fiat terms**.

## 7. Common Beginner Mistakes

1. **Chasing yield without reading risk**: treating structured products like savings and taking on risks you don't understand.
2. **Ignoring price movement**: earning 8% in coin terms while the coin falls 20% is a real loss.
3. **Concentrating everything in one product**: liquidity and risk both pile up; redemption limits leave you stuck.
4. **Assuming "Earn = principal-protected"**&#8203;: there's no deposit insurance in crypto — losses are possible.
5. **Forgetting to rebalance**: parking funds in low-yield flexible options for months is also a cost.
