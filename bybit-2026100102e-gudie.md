## 1. What Is TradFi?

**TradFi = Traditional Finance** — crypto's shorthand for the entire financial system that exists outside crypto. It includes:

- **Forex**: currency pairs such as EUR/USD.
- **Commodities**: gold, silver, crude oil, and more.
- **Indices and equities**: major stock indices and single stocks (often offered as CFDs).
- **Traditional market structure**: brokers, banks, clearing houses, regulators, and fixed trading sessions.

### TradFi vs. DeFi

| Factor | TradFi | DeFi |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Operating entities | Banks, brokers, exchanges | Smart contracts and protocols |
| Custody | Institution-held | Self-custody (wallet) |
| Trading hours | Market sessions (not 24/7) | Around the clock |
| Regulation | Heavy, well-defined rules | Lighter, varies by region |
| Typical products | Forex, gold, stocks, bonds | Lending, liquidity mining, DEXs |

**They aren't opposites**: a wave of "bridging" products now brings traditional assets onto crypto platforms — Bybit TradFi is a prime example.
